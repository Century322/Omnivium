Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Data Collection

Omnivium collects minimal data necessary to provide our services:

  • Account credentials: Username and encrypted password for authentication. Passwords are never stored in plain text.
  • Messages: End-to-end encrypted messages are stored on the Matrix homeserver. Omnivium cannot read your encrypted messages.
  • Device information: Device model, OS version, and app version for crash reporting and compatibility.
  • Usage analytics: Anonymous, aggregated usage statistics to improve the app experience.

Data Usage

Your data is used solely to:

  • Provide and maintain the Omnivium messaging service
  • Deliver AI assistant features you explicitly enable
  • Send push notifications you have opted into
  • Improve app performance and stability through crash reporting

We never sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

Data Storage

  • Messages: Stored on your chosen Matrix homeserver with end-to-end encryption
  • Local data: Stored securely on your device using platform keychain/keystore encryption
  • AI conversations: Stored locally on your device; not sent to third parties unless you explicitly use cloud AI features
  • Crash reports: Sent to Sentry (our crash reporting provider) with sensitive data filtered out

Data Protection

  • End-to-end encryption for all messages using the Matrix protocol (Olm/Megolm)
  • SRP (Secure Remote Password) protocol for authentication
  • Biometric authentication (fingerprint/face recognition) for app access
  • Secure storage using platform-native encryption (Keychain/Keystore)
  • SSL certificate pinning for API communications
  • TOTP two-factor authentication available

Data Deletion

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from Settings > Account > Delete Account. This action:

  • Permanently deletes your account from the Matrix homeserver
  • Removes all local data from your device
  • Cannot be undone

Third-Party Services

Omnivium uses the following third-party services:

  • Matrix Protocol: Decentralized messaging infrastructure
  • Firebase: Push notification delivery
  • Sentry: Crash reporting (with sensitive data filtering)
  • Supabase: Backend services for account management

Children's Privacy

Omnivium is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Policy Updates

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Significant changes will be notified through the app. Continued use of Omnivium after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
